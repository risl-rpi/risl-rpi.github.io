## Shiqian Xu

Shiqian Xu is a Ph.D. student in Mathematics at Rensselaer Polytechnic Institute (RPI). Her research focuses on neural network reachability analysis and formal verification under hardware faults. She received her M.S. in Computational and Applied Mathematics from the University of Chicago and her B.S. in Mathematics from RPI. Outside of research, she enjoys board games, hiking, painting, playing Go, and reading.
